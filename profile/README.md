## From idea to live strategy

| 1. Describe it | 2. Test it | 3. Run it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tell your agent the setup, market, and risk you have in mind. | Backtest the rules before any capital is put to work. | Deploy to managed infrastructure and monitor it around the clock. |

Superior Trade handles the operational layer between an idea and a running strategy: hosting, exchange connectivity, deployment state, execution logs, and ongoing operation.

## Your strategy. Your approval. Your controls.

- **Test before deployment.** Compare backtest results and refine the rules before going live.
- **You approve live trading.** An agent must show the deployment details and receive explicit confirmation before it starts trading real funds.
- **Managed, isolated operation.** Each deployment runs independently, with status and logs available when you need them.
- **Stop when you choose.** Inspect and control running strategies from the Superior Trade experience.

Trading involves risk. Backtests are simulations, not guarantees of future performance.
